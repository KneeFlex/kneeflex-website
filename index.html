02 — OUR STORYFROM EXPERIENCE TO PURPOSE

Recovery made
it personal.

After foot surgery in Australia, our founder relied on a knee scooter to get to class, visit the library, and move through everyday life independently.

That freedom mattered. So did the pressure on one knee, the bumps, and the effort of staying steady. KneeFlex grew from that experience: a commitment to rethink how recovery feels.

05 — FAQ

Frequently
Asked Questions

Who is KneeFlex designed for?

People who need to keep weight off one foot or ankle during recovery, while retaining knee and hip function.

Can I adjust the perch?

Yes. The pelvic perch is designed to adjust in height, so you can find a position that suits your body.

How does it share the load?

The perch supports part of your weight at the pelvis. The longer cradle spreads support from knee to shin.
